A standalone HTML document with its own typography. It demonstrates that HTML files keep their original look — completely different from the app's design system.

client ── HTTPS ──▶ reverse proxy ──▶ rymark:8080 │ │ │ ├─ /healthz (public) │ ├─ /api/auth/* (rate limited) │ └─ /content/* (auth gate)

Everything under /content/ is denied to unauthenticated callers, including direct URLs, images and the search index.
